Terms of engagement

Conditions
01

Scope. The package you choose is our guide. You can add services any time at the rates above, and step back from one with the notice in clause 11.

02

Payment. The website build is paid before we start, and so is other one-time work. Anything over $1,000 can be split 50% up front, balance on delivery. Monthly services are billed in advance each cycle.

03

Revisions. Rounds are listed per service, and revisions are free for now — we’d rather get it right than argue about it. New pages, services or a fresh direction are quoted before anyone starts.

04

Timeline. Dates start when payment clears and we have your final content. If something on your side runs late, we shift the dates with you, day for day.

05

Your side. Your brand assets, copy and imagery, plus access to the domain, Google Business Profile, ad accounts and phone system — and one consolidated round of feedback within 3 working days so we can keep moving.

06

Accounts & ownership. Your domain, Google Business Profile, ad accounts and phone numbers always stay in your name. Everything we deliver is yours once the final invoice is paid.

07

AI & call handling. Some deliverables include AI-generated material, and an AI receptionist may answer your calls. Recording consent is handled where you’re based — we’ll flag anything you need to put in place.

08

No ranking guarantee. Search, Maps and AI answers are run by platforms nobody controls. We promise the work and the reporting, not a position — and we’ll tell you honestly what’s realistic.

09

Warranty. If something we built breaks in the first 30 days after launch, we fix it at no charge. New content and third-party outages sit outside that.

10

Liability. Our total liability is limited to the fees paid in the previous 3 months, and we’re not liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11

Cancellation. Work paid up front isn’t refundable once it’s underway, but anything not yet done is returned to you. Monthly services need 30 days’ written notice — no lock-in, no hard feelings.
